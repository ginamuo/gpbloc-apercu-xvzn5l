02Intellectual property

The text, photos, videos, logos and 3D models on this site belong to GP Bloc inc. Some photos come from the Pexels and Unsplash libraries and are used under their licences. Any reproduction requires GP Bloc’s written consent.

You can also follow GP Bloc on Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn.

03Privacy officer

GP Bloc inc. is committed to protecting the personal information it collects. Under Quebec’s Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector, the person with the highest authority in the company, Michel Perreault, owner and general manager, serves as privacy officer.

For any request, call us at 1-844-901-2562 or write to us at 131 rue Principale, Saint-Esprit, Quebec J0K 2L0. You can also reach us any time through our contact form.

04What we collect

We only collect what your project needs:

  • Contact details: name, phone, email and, for businesses, the organization’s name.
  • Your project: type of structure, products, site address, access conditions, attached photos or plans.
  • Job applications: résumé and details provided in the Careers form.
  • Client portal: login details, rentals, quotes and invoices.

05How we use it

  • To prepare your quotes and work orders.
  • To deliver, install, collect and invoice your blocks.
  • To answer your questions and review job applications.
  • To improve the site, only with your consent.

06Your consent

We collect your information with your consent when you fill in a form. You can withdraw it at any time and we will stop using it, subject to our legal obligations, such as keeping invoices.

07Who we share it with

We never sell your information. We only share it when your project requires it:

  • with partner plants and carriers, to produce and deliver your order;
  • with technical providers such as our web host, bound by confidentiality agreements.

Some providers may store data outside Quebec. In that case we carry out a privacy impact assessment before sharing anything, as the law requires.

08How long we keep it

We keep your information as long as needed for the purposes above, then for the period tax laws require for accounting records. Quotes that don’t go ahead are deleted after two years, and job applications after one year.

09Security and incidents

We protect your information with safeguards suited to its sensitivity, including access limited to staff who need it and encrypted connections.

If a confidentiality incident poses a risk of serious harm, we notify the people affected and Quebec’s Commission d’accès à l’information, and we record it in our incident register.

10Your rights

  • Access the information we hold about you.
  • Have it corrected if it is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Withdraw your consent or ask for it to be deleted.
  • Get a copy in a structured, commonly used technological format.

We respond to every request within 30 days. You may also file a complaint with Quebec’s Commission d’accès à l’information.

11Cookies and third-party services

The site uses a handful of essential cookies and only loads third-party services, such as map tiles, once you agree. See our cookie policy for details.

12Updates

We may update this policy as our services or the law change. The date of the latest update appears under the table of contents.
