01What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small file a website saves in your browser. Our site also uses your browser’s local storage, which works the same way. We call both cookies here.

02Essential cookies

These keep the site working and don’t require your consent. They are never used to track you from one site to another.

NamePurposeDuration
gpbloc-temoinsRemembers your cookie choice.Until you change it
gpbloc-projetKeeps your “My project” list and your Bloc Builder build.Until you clear the list
gpbloc-clientKeeps your client portal session.Until you close the tab

03Third-party services, with consent

The site’s maps use OpenStreetMap and CARTO tiles. These services receive your IP address when they send the map images. They only load once you agree, or when you click “Show the map”.

The site has no audience measurement tools and no advertising.
