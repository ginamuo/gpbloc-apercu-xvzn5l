    Asphalt plant operator assistant

    Labelle, QC

    Reporting to the crew lead, you help run the asphalt plant’s day-to-day operations.

    Role details

    What you’ll do

    • Load trucks
    • Oversee bitumen tanker unloading
    • Maintain production equipment
    • Troubleshoot and repair breakdowns

    What you’ll need

    • Industrial mechanics DEP, an asset
    • Experience in a similar role
    • Basic mechanics, electrical and welding
    • Comfortable at heights and in bad weather
  • 02

    Head of the laboratory

    You make sure every product leaving our quarries and plants meets spec.

    Role details

    What you’ll do

    • Test raw materials and production runs
    • Sample on site and run lab tests
    • Report non-conformities
    • Improve practices and procedures

    What you’ll need

    • Civil or geological engineering DEC, or equivalent
    • Field concrete testing technician, level 1
    • ASP construction safety card
    • Class 5 driver’s licence
  • 03

    Concrete keeper

    You make sure nothing lands in fresh concrete until it has set.

    Role details

    What you’ll do

    • Watch over fresh concrete surfaces
    • Work alongside the concrete crew

    What you’ll need

    • Construction site safety card, required
    • Able to stay on your feet for long hours
  • 04

    Accounting technician

    Reporting to the finance director, you own month-end and year-end for the construction division.

    Role details

    What you’ll do

    • Bill clients, progress draws included
    • Run the full accounts payable cycle
    • Track subcontractor compliance
    • Produce month-end financial statements

    What you’ll need

    • Accounting DEP or DEC
    • Three to five years of experience
    • Strong Office skills
    • Fluent French, working English
