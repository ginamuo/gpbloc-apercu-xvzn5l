Products & specs

What do the different MPa ratings mean?

MPa (megapascals) measures concrete’s compressive strength. The higher the number, the stronger the concrete, so a structural job calls for a higher rating than a landscaping one.

Why is air added to concrete?

Entrained air creates tiny bubbles that help concrete survive our freeze-thaw cycles, which cuts the risk of cracks caused by expanding water.

How do I protect my blocks over the years?

A sealer does the job well, and it comes in a wide range of colours to match your blocks.

How do I choose the right product for my project?

Start with your town’s rules, then pin down the use, since a retaining wall and a decorative feature don’t need the same concrete. Factor in strength (MPa), climate, soil and the look you want. An advisor can point you in the right direction.

How high can a block wall go?

It depends on the block, the soil and the load behind it. In Quebec, any wall over 1.2 m (4 ft) generally has to be designed or approved by an engineer.

At what height do I need an engineer’s plan?

The most common rule in Quebec sets the line at 1.2 m (4 ft). A plan may also be required if the wall holds back a slope, a driveway or a parking area, if the soil is unstable or close to a foundation, or simply if your town says so.

Delivery & equipment

How many blocks fit on your trucks?

A five-axle rig can haul up to 90,000 lb. Our boom truck carries up to 30,360 lb and our lowboy trailer up to 44,000 lb.

How big are your trailers?

Our standard trailers run 48 to 53 ft long and 102 in wide, with a deck about 5 ft high. Empty, they weigh 10,000 to 15,000 lb, and their maximum load ranges from 45,000 to 90,000 lb depending on the type and the number of axles.

How far can your cranes reach?

Our long-reach crane covers 30 ft horizontally and 40 ft vertically, and lifts up to 7,500 lb with precision, even on a tight site.

Can you set blocks in the water?

Yes. We place blocks right into the water for docks, shoreline stabilization and dykes, working around depth, current and site access.

Can you get onto rough terrain?

Yes. Our specialized equipment gets us into hard-to-reach areas, and our team assesses every site before the job.

How much clearance do you need?

Our forklifts need a lane at least 9 ft wide to operate safely.

What area do you serve?

We serve all of Quebec and Ontario. Through our partner network, we can also point you to the nearest plant if you’d rather pick your products up yourself.

Installation & site prep

How do I prepare the ground?

The ground has to be level, compacted and able to carry the weight of the blocks. Depending on the wall height and the terrain, a compacted gravel base or a concrete slab may be recommended. Our team can assess it and take care of the excavation.

Do you do excavation?

We connect you with our partners for excavation and site prep, and help you plan the project itself.

Do you deliver and install the blocks?

Yes. Our machinery is built for hauling concrete blocks, so we offer turnkey trucking, handling and placement anywhere in Quebec and Ontario.

How long do unloading and placement take?

It depends on the order, the block type and the equipment on site. Get the site ready ahead of time by freeing up the area and setting aside a drop zone trucks can reach.

Planning & support

Do you supply engineer’s plans?

Not directly, but we connect you with qualified engineers. A plan is required for any wall over 1.2 m (4 ft), for walls carrying an extra load, and for projects that need municipal approval.

What does a concrete products broker do?

We sit between the plants and you. We pick the best-suited products, optimize deliveries and coordinate every step, saving you time and money.

What problems does a broker save me?

People who source blocks on their own mostly run into hard-to-reach plants and messy coordination. One call to us takes care of both.

How can I pay?

We take bank transfers, Interac e-Transfer, cheques and credit cards.

Do you work evenings and weekends?

Yes, holidays included. Rates are available on request.

How do you help me cut costs?

We tighten every step: technical advice, efficient trucking, specialized equipment and a single point of contact. Your logistics get simpler and your site moves faster.
