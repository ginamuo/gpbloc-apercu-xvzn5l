Your yard, built one block at a time

Homeowners

FIG. 01 · Concrete block landscaping

Maybe your slope slides a little further every spring, or cars keep chewing up the lawn while the woodpile sprawls across the yard. At home, too, some problems call for a solid answer. Concrete blocks are one of them, as long as you pick the right model and get it delivered to the right spot.

1,220 mm

That’s where we come in. You describe the project, we recommend the right block, then we handle the trucking, the lifting and, if you like, the installation. You get the same machinery and know-how we bring to major job sites, with the same care for your backyard.

Your projects

Four projects, one tough block.

01

Hold back a slope

A sagging bank or a sloped yard gets a lot easier to live with once a concrete block retaining wall goes in. The sheer weight of the blocks keeps the wall stable, and staggering the joints builds it to last for years. Depending on the height, your town may ask for a permit or an engineer’s drawings, and we’ll walk you through the right questions before you start.

02

Shape your yard

Decorative blocks and Grani-Blocs turn into low walls, garden edging, terraced steps and outdoor benches. They give a landscape real character and shrug off our winters, and their finishes look as much at home in a modern backyard as on a wooded lot.

03

Mark out and protect your land

Whether cars keep cutting across your lawn or a cottage road needs closing off, a few well-placed blocks settle it for good. In the parking area, our concrete wheel stops guide every car into its spot.

04

Store your materials

Blocks set in a U make sturdy bays for firewood, topsoil, gravel or compost, easy to fill by wheelbarrow or tractor. The yard gets its order back, and your materials stop creeping across it season after season.

FAQ

Good questions, straight answers.

01Can the truck get into my yard?
Placeholder content

In most cases, yes. When you request a quote you tell us how wide the access is and what the ground is like, and we check it all before confirming delivery.

02Do I need a permit for a retaining wall?

It depends on your town and on the height of the wall. Check with your local planning department before you order, since engineered drawings may be required above a certain height.

03Does the ground need to be prepared before delivery?

Level, compacted ground makes placement easier and keeps the wall stable. If you book our installation service, we’ll tell you exactly how to prepare it.

04Can I order a single block?
Placeholder content

Yes. Trucking, based on distance, is simply added to your quote.

05Do you deliver to my area?

We deliver anywhere in Quebec and Ontario. Trucking varies with distance and is itemized in your quote.

06How long can I rent blocks for?
Placeholder content

Anywhere from a few days to several months, depending on your project. The pickup date is set out in your quote.
